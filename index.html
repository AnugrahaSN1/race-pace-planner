How it works. Predictions use Riegel's formula, T₂ = T₁ × (D₂ / D₁)1.06, the standard way to project a race time to another distance. A goal at or slower than the prediction is realistic, up to 3% faster is ambitious, and more than 3% faster is unlikely.

Limits. It assumes a flat course, similar weather and training suited to the distance. Projections from a 5K to a marathon are the least reliable, because endurance matters more over longer distances.
